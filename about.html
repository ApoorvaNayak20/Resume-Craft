About Resume Craft

A simpler way to present your skills, learning and experience.

Our purpose

Resume Craft is designed to help students, freshers and job seekers organize their information into a clear, professional resume. It brings information entry, template selection, preview and export into one guided workflow.

Our approach

We believe a good resume should be truthful, readable and relevant to the opportunity. Our templates favour clean typography, clear sections and practical customization rather than distracting decoration.

What you can do

  • Build and edit a resume.
  • Choose from professional layouts and colours.
  • Preview your document before exporting.
  • Save different resume versions.
  • Get basic guidance while writing.
