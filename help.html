How do I create a resume?

Open Dashboard, select Personal & Education, complete required fields, save and preview.

Can I leave optional fields blank?

Yes. Optional information can be left blank. NIL can be used where offered.

How do I download?

Open Download and choose PDF, PNG or Print.
